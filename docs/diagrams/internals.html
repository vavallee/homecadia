Two things this drawing deliberately does not pretend to know: the case has no battery compartment and no sensor mount — both were left open for the test fit — and the driver board and panel outlines are sketched rather than measured, because those dimensions were never captured in the repo. Everything tagged geometry came out of the STL files.

What the arrangement forces

  • The cell sets the length. Tray plus cell is 104.5 mm end to end. That single sum is why the shells were cut at x = 24.73 mm and stretched 7 mm, and why only 2.1 mm of slack remains.
  • The panel bridges both zones. Its 68 mm aperture spans the tray and the cell, so the glass has to sit in front of everything — nothing may stand proud enough to push it off the aperture.
  • The XIAO's underside is working surface. Battery pads, the MTDI divider node and the encoder-switch pad all live there, so it cannot be face-down against the tray with its wiring trapped.
  • The SHT40 wants distance and air. Mounted near the MCU or out of the vent path, self-heating skews every reading the device exists to take.
