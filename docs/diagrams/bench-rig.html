Before power

  1. USB out while you wire. Plug it back in last.
  2. Wire by the silkscreen name, not by position counting: RST, CS, BUSY, DC, SCK, MOSI, 3V3, GND. The labels are on the board's back and mirror when it lies sockets-up — a one-off or mirrored shift is the classic mistake with jumpers into a header.
  3. FPC before power: flip the connector latch up, slide the cable in contacts-down until it stops, press the latch closed. Never insert or remove the cable powered.
  4. 3V3 row is now shared three ways (XIAO pin, SHT40 VCC, driver board) — all in the same 5-hole row.
  5. Battery JST and the driver board's power switch stay untouched.

What success looks like

  1. Within a few seconds of boot the panel flashes black/white (a full refresh) and draws the commissioning screen: "homecadia — Add via Matter" with the QR and manual code.
  2. ePaper is not a live screen — it only changes when the firmware pushes a refresh, and the readings view updates on the same delta gate as the radio report.
  3. Nothing at all: check the 3V3 and GND jumpers, then the FPC latch. If still dead, try the driver board's power switch — whether it gates socket power is unverified.
  4. Garbage or partial image: SCK (D8) / MOSI (D10) seating. Hangs mid-refresh: BUSY (D2).
  5. The console tells the truth either way — same capture as step 1.
