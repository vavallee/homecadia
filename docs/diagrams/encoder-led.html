Sensor-01 Encoder & LED

Step 4 adds the two input/output parts the enclosure needs: the EC11 rotary encoder that drives the three display views, and the status LED. Four wires, one resistor, and one joint that cannot be breadboarded — the encoder's push switch lives on an underside pad, not on the header.

Power — unchanged from step 3

LiPo or USB-Ceither; both is fine
3V3 logicLED and encoder both run off it
no new supplynothing here draws more than ~4 mA

Neither part needs its own rail. The encoder is passive — it only shorts contacts to ground — and the LED is driven straight off a GPIO through a series resistor. Total added idle draw is zero: the encoder's internal pull-ups only sink current while a contact is closed, and the LED is off except during commissioning and low battery (led.cpp).

Pin assignment changed 2026-08-25/26, drawing updated to match: encoder A → D9, encoder B → D6, LED → D7. D7 was the original encoder-A pin; on both driver boards it reads 0 V once the SPI bus is up (unexplained — field-notes §16), so the LED, an output, took it and the encoder input moved to D6. A and B are then chosen so clockwise advances the view. Also from the bench: EC11 pins do not seat in a breadboard — the blades are ~0.6 mm and neither breadboard springs nor Dupont sockets grip them. Solder three short leads to the pins and put those in the breadboard; the encoder produced zero clean detents until that was done.

This wiring is bench-only. In the assembled unit the encoder does not connect to the XIAO header at all — the ePaper driver board breaks out D4–D7 and D9 as its own through-holes, and A/B solder there (assembly.md). Only the SW-to-TP6 lead is the same in both builds.

What is deliberately absent

Part you might expectWhy it is not here
Pull-up resistors on A, B, SW The C6's internal pull-ups are enabled in firmware for all three (ec11.c:73, button_gpio.c:136). External ones would only add a parallel leak path.
Debounce capacitors on A and B Debounce is a 4-state Gray-code transition table, not a timer or an RC network. Contact bounce produces invalid state transitions that the table discards, so the encoder consumes nothing while idle (ec11.h:1-4).
Debounce on the push switch Handled in software by espressif/button.
A second LED The XIAO already has a yellow one on GPIO15 — but it is active low, sinks through an on-board 1.5 kΩ to 3V3, and GPIO15 is a C6 strapping pin. It is left high-Z and unused (pinmap.md).

Sizing R3

Driven from a 3.3 V push-pull output, so R = (3.3 − Vf) / I. The LED is a status indicator seen through diffused plastic, not a lamp — a few milliamps is plenty, and keeping it low protects the GPIO.

LED colourTypical VfR3Resulting current
Red~2.0 V330 Ω≈ 3.9 mA
Yellow / amber~2.1 V330 Ω≈ 3.6 mA
Green~2.2 V220 Ω≈ 5.0 mA
Blue / white~3.0 V100 Ω≈ 3.0 mA
Blue and white are a poor choice on a 3.3 V rail. With Vf ≈ 3.0 V there is only ~0.3 V of headroom, so brightness swings hard with part-to-part Vf spread and with the rail sagging as the cell discharges. Pick red, amber or green from the assortment. The Vf figures above are typical values for the LED chemistry, not measured — Chanzon does not publish a per-colour datasheet for the kit. Measure the drop across the LED once it is lit and adjust R3 if the current is off target.

What success looks like

  1. One detent, one step. Turning the shaft one click changes the view exactly once: readings → diagnostics → settings and back (ui.cpp:108-127). Two steps per detent, or steps in both directions from one click, means the Gray-code decode is seeing a pin that is not making clean contact — check the C-to-ground wire first.
  2. Clockwise advances. If it goes backwards, swap the A and B wires. This is the open HW-VERIFY row; the direction has never been observed on hardware.
  3. Push does nothing outside settings. That is correct — on_push() returns early unless the settings view is showing (ui.cpp:129-134). Rotate to settings first, then push to enter the menu, push again to edit.
  4. Ten-second hold factory-resets. Console prints W (…) ui: Encoder held 10s: factory reset and the device reboots into commissioning. Confirm this before the unit is commissioned to anything you care about.
  5. LED blinks at 1 Hz while uncommissioned (500 ms on, 500 ms off) and pulses briefly every 10 s below 10 % battery. Solid on or solid off in either state means the wrong leg is grounded or R3 is open.

Bring-up rows this closes

  • GPIO6 (MTCK) wakes the C6 from deep sleep on encoder press — needs the TP6 lead in place and a deep-sleep build; the wake source is registered but has never fired on hardware.
  • Encoder clockwise direction (ec11.h HW-VERIFY).
  • Encoder knob bore against the 6 mm D-shaft — mechanical, and only testable once a printed set exists.
