Sensor-01 Bench Verify

What every wire on the bench is supposed to connect, and what a meter should read at each node with the PPK2 as the battery. Pins come from firmware/sensor-01/main/app_config.h, the file that is actually flashed — not from assembly.md, whose encoder/LED and divider pins are stale.

How to use this page

  • Match by node, not by row number. Row numbers in the drawings are the drawing's own; your board's rows differ. A node is "everything that must read the same voltage".
  • Measure in-circuit. DC volts, black probe on the PPK2 GND terminal, red on the metal of the lead or pad. Never unplug a wire to read it — a floating wire reads whatever charge is trapped on it.
  • PPK2 is the battery. Source Meter, 4000 mV, VOUT → BAT+, GND → BAT−, VIN unused. USB out of the XIAO. No cell anywhere.
  • Expected values assume 4000 mV. SENSE reads 1.90 V on a 10 MΩ meter (0.476 × source) — the meter loads the 1 MΩ lower leg. The ADC sees 2.00 V.

1 · Power and battery divider — the node under investigation

The one confirmed fault (2026-09-09): BAT+ pad read 3.97 V while the divider top read 3.36 V with the source at 4000 mV. At 2 µA through the divider no joint drops 0.61 V, so those are two different nodes: R1's top leg is on the 3V3 rail (regulator output, held at 3.3 V) instead of BAT+. Every reading of "battery voltage" this session has been the rail.

The decisive test is the sweep, not any single point. Set 3400 → 3700 → 4000 (slider, not the number box) and re-read BAT+ and SENSE at each. BAT+ must follow the source; SENSE must follow at 0.476×. A node that stays at 3.3 V while the source moves is the regulator, and that is where a leg is wrongly landed. Two nodes within 0.2 V of each other are indistinguishable by a one-point check — that is how the 2026-09-02 "verified" reading was actually the rail.
